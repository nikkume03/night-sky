Constellations

Three patterns of stars you can find on a clear winter night.

Orion

The hunter. Find the three stars in a row that make his belt.

Ursa Major

The Great Bear. Its seven brightest stars form the Big Dipper.

Cassiopeia

A bright W shape in the northern sky that never sets from many places.

Quick facts

Click a question to open the answer.

Why do stars twinkle?

Starlight passes through moving layers of air in our atmosphere, which bends the light slightly and makes it flicker.

How far away is the Moon?

About 384,000 km. Light from the Moon takes just over one second to reach us.

What is a shooting star?

A tiny piece of space dust or rock burning up as it enters the atmosphere. It is not a star at all.

What causes the aurora?

Charged particles from the Sun hit gases in Earth's atmosphere near the poles and make them glow.
